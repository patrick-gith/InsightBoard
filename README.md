## 🇬🇧 English

### 📊 InsightBoard: turn your sales data into decisions

#### In one sentence

**InsightBoard automatically analyzes your sales files (CSV) and generates a complete dashboard and a professional PDF report in seconds — with zero technical configuration.**

---

#### The problem we solve

Every business has sales data — point-of-sale exports, Excel files, ERP reports. But this data often stays **unused**:

- You need an analyst to build charts
- Pivot tables take hours
- Monthly reports are repetitive and costly
- Decisions are made without clear numbers

**Result**: wasted time, missed opportunities, a blurry view of the business.

---

#### Our solution

InsightBoard is a web tool that **automates sales analysis** end to end.

**1. Upload your file**
Drop your CSV (POS export, accounting file, ERP data) onto the home page. No format imposed, no columns to rename.

**2. Analysis happens automatically**
The system **detects** your columns on its own:
- Transaction date
- Amount / price / total
- Quantity
- Product or item
- City / region
- Currency (€, $, FCFA, MAD…)

No configuration, no training required.

**3. You instantly get**

**A visual dashboard** with:
- **6 key indicators**: total revenue, average order, total quantity, best-selling product, top city, number of transactions
- **4 interactive charts**: revenue by city, top 10 products, revenue breakdown by product, monthly trend

**A professional PDF report**: same content, formatted for printing or emailing to your teams, investors, or partners.

Chaque entreprise dispose de données de ventes — exports de caisse, fichiers Excel, rapports d'ERP. Mais ces données restent souvent **inexploitées** :

- Il faut un analyste pour créer les graphiques

InsightBoard est un outil web qui **automatise l'analyse de vos ventes** de bout en bout.

**1. Importez votre fichier**

**2. L'analyse se fait automatiquement**
Le système **reconnaît tout seul** vos colonnes :

- Devise (€, $, FCFA, MAD…)
